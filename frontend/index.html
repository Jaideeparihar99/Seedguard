BEFORE YOU SOW
Record a small seed-sample check
Count what grew from a sample and compare it with the packet statement, if shown. This records your observation; it does not grade the packet or the whole lot.
✳

    
Simple observation guide
  1. Take a random sample from the opened seed lot.
  2. Place it on a clean, moist towel or cloth. Keep it moist, not waterlogged.
  3. Check at a time suitable for your crop and count normal seedlings. Ask a local agriculture expert if you are unsure what to count.
Method and timing vary by crop. An ICAR soybean advisory gives a soybean-specific example ↗

    

      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    

    

    

    

    
A small home sample is not an official germination test and may not represent the whole lot or predict field emergence. For a formal result, contact an authorized seed testing laboratory.
